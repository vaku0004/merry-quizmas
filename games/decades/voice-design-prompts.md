# Narrator voice for Decades Showdown: ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts

Where: ElevenLabs → Voices → Create a voice → Voice Design. Paste a voice description, paste the preview text, generate, listen to all variants, save the best one, and send the Voice ID. The game calls the narrator "DJ Rewind" (changeable in `games/decades/theme.py`).

## Voice description

### A. Retro radio DJ (main choice)

```
An energetic, charismatic radio DJ in his forties with a smooth, warm baritone and a neutral American accent. He sounds like the host of a hit countdown show on a 1980s or 1990s radio station: upbeat, fun and confident, with big-smile energy and a little showbiz sparkle. Clear diction and a lively but easy-to-follow pace for a room full of coworkers. He can build suspense before a reveal and get playfully dramatic for surprises, but he never shouts. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

### B. Retro radio DJ (female)

```
An energetic, charismatic radio DJ in her thirties with a bright, warm voice and a neutral American accent. She sounds like the host of a hit countdown show on a 1990s radio station: upbeat, witty and confident, with a smile you can hear. Clear diction and a lively but easy-to-follow pace for a room full of coworkers. She can build suspense before a reveal and get playfully dramatic for surprises, but she never shouts. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

## Preview text

```
Welcome to Victoria's Decades Showdown! Tonight, our teams travel back to the eighties, the nineties and the two thousands, for glory and for prizes. But watch out for the tangled tape... and for the time machine! Here is your question: Which round, yellow, dot-eating arcade character first appeared in 1980? The answer is... Pac-Man! Way to go, Team Cassettes!
```

## What to listen for

- Every word is clear on laptop speakers in a room with chatter.
- Fun and energetic, but not tiring to listen to for an hour.
- Dramatic for surprises without shouting.
- No echo, music or background noise.
