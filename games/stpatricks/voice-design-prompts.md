# Narrator voice for Pot o' Gold: ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts

Where: ElevenLabs → Voices → Create a voice → Voice Design. Paste a voice description, paste the preview text, generate, listen to all variants, save the best one, and send the Voice ID. The game calls the narrator "Lucky Liam" (the name in Voice settings can be changed in `games/stpatricks/theme.py`).

A real, warm Irish voice, not a cartoon leprechaun: the game is played in offices around the world, and a caricature can feel like a joke at Irish people's expense.

## Voice description

### A. Irish storyteller (main choice)

```
A warm, friendly Irish man in his fifties with a gentle, genuine Dublin accent and a smooth, slightly gravelly baritone. He sounds like a much-loved storyteller in a cosy pub on St. Patrick's Day: witty, twinkly and kind, with a soft chuckle in his voice. Natural, authentic Irish speech, never a cartoon or a stage accent. Clear diction and a relaxed, unhurried pace that people from any country can follow. He can turn playfully mischievous for tricky moments and build a little suspense before a reveal. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

### B. Irish radio host (female)

```
A bright, cheerful Irish woman in her thirties with a soft, genuine accent from the west of Ireland and a warm, clear mezzo voice. She sounds like a favourite morning radio host on St. Patrick's Day: quick-witted, playful and kind, with a smile you can hear. Natural, authentic Irish speech, never a cartoon or a stage accent. Clear diction and an easy pace that people from any country can follow. She can tease a little and build suspense before a reveal. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

## Preview text

```
Welcome to Victoria's Pot o' Gold! Tonight, our teams compete for glory... and for gold. Pick a question from the board, and you have thirty seconds to answer. But watch out for the leprechaun... and for the rainbow. Here is your question: Kissing which stone is said to give you the gift of the gab? The answer is... the Blarney Stone! Well done, Team Shamrocks!
```

## What to listen for

- Every word is clear for people from any country, on laptop speakers in a room with chatter.
- The accent sounds real and warm, not exaggerated.
- Playful for the leprechaun, never mocking.
- No echo, music or background noise.
