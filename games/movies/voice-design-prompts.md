# Narrator voice for Movie Night: ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts

Where: ElevenLabs → Voices → Create a voice → Voice Design. Paste a voice description, paste the preview text, generate, listen to all variants, save the best one, and send the Voice ID. The game calls the narrator "Marquee Max" (changeable in `games/movies/theme.py`); a good name in ElevenLabs is the same.

## Voice description

### A. Movie-trailer voice (main choice, male)

```
A deep, rich, cinematic male voice in his fifties with a neutral American accent, like the narrator of a classic Hollywood movie trailer, but warm and friendly rather than serious. He has a smooth, resonant baritone, a playful sense of drama and a wink in his voice. Clear diction and an unhurried pace that a room full of coworkers can follow. He builds suspense before a reveal, loves a big dramatic pause, and sounds delighted when a team gets it right. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

### B. Old-Hollywood host (female)

```
A glamorous, warm woman in her forties with a smooth, velvety voice and a light mid-Atlantic accent, like a host on the red carpet of a classic Hollywood premiere. She is charming, witty and a little theatrical, with a smile you can hear. Clear diction and a relaxed pace that a room full of coworkers can follow. She builds suspense before a reveal and sounds delighted when a team gets it right. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

## Preview text

```
Welcome to Victoria's Movie Night! Tonight, our teams compete for glory... and for popcorn. But watch out for the plot twist... and for the popcorn bucket! Here is your question: In Finding Nemo, what kind of fish is Nemo? The answer is... a clownfish! Bravo, Team Popcorn!
```

## What to listen for

- Every word is clear on laptop speakers in a room with chatter.
- Dramatic and fun, not gloomy: it is a party game.
- Big pauses work, but the pace should not drag over an hour.
- No echo, music or background noise.
