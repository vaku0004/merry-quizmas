# Narrator voice for Countdown Quiz: ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts

Where: ElevenLabs → Voices → Create a voice → Voice Design. Paste a voice description, paste the preview text, generate, listen to all variants, save the best one, and send the Voice ID. The game calls the narrator "Midnight Mae" (changeable in `games/newyear/theme.py`); a good name in ElevenLabs is the same.

## Voice description

### A. Countdown party host (main choice, female)

```
A bright, warm, sparkling female voice in her thirties with a neutral American accent, like the host of a glamorous New Year's Eve countdown party on live TV. She is upbeat and charming, with a smile you can hear and real excitement as midnight gets closer, but never shrill. Clear diction and a relaxed pace that a room full of coworkers can follow. She builds suspense before a reveal, loves a dramatic pause, and sounds delighted when a team gets it right. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

### B. Velvet late-night host (male)

```
A smooth, warm male voice in his forties with a neutral American accent, like a charming late-night radio host counting down to midnight at an elegant party. He is relaxed and witty, with a playful sense of drama and a little sparkle in his voice. Clear diction and an unhurried pace that a room full of coworkers can follow. He builds suspense before a reveal and sounds delighted when a team gets it right. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

## Preview text

```
Welcome to Victoria's Countdown Quiz! Tonight, our teams compete to end the year... on top. But watch out for the party popper... and for the fortune cookie! Here is your question: In Spain, how many grapes do people eat at midnight? The answer is... twelve! Happy New Year, Team Confetti!
```

## What to listen for

- Every word is clear on laptop speakers in a room with chatter.
- Festive and fun, not tired or sleepy: it is the last party of the year.
- Big pauses work, but the pace should not drag over an hour.
- No echo, music or background noise.
