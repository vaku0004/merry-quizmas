# Narrator voice for Merry Quizmas: ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts

Where: ElevenLabs → Voices → Create a voice → Voice Design. Paste a voice description, paste the preview text, generate, listen to all variants, save the best one. Then copy its Voice ID (it is not secret, it can go into chat).

## Voice description (pick one)

### A. Grandfather by the fireplace (main choice)

```
A warm, jolly storyteller in his sixties with a rich, slightly husky baritone and a gentle Canadian accent. He sounds like a beloved grandfather reading a Christmas story by the fireplace: cosy, twinkly and full of good humour, with a soft chuckle in his voice. Clear diction and an unhurried pace, perfect for reading trivia questions aloud to a room. For mischievous moments he can drop into a playful, slightly sly tone, but he is never scary. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

### B. Game-show host with Christmas spirit (more energy)

```
A cheerful, charismatic male game-show host in his forties with a bright, friendly baritone and a light Canadian accent. He sounds like he is hosting a festive TV quiz on Christmas Eve: upbeat, confident and genuinely delighted to be there, with a warm smile you can hear. Crisp, clear diction and a lively but never rushed pace, easy to follow in a noisy office party. He can build suspense before a big reveal and turn playfully sly for tricky moments. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

### C. Storyteller grandmother (a female voice)

```
A warm, kind-hearted storyteller in her sixties with a soft, rounded alto voice and a gentle Canadian accent. She sounds like a beloved grandmother reading a Christmas tale to the family by the tree: cosy, sparkling and quietly funny, with a little laugh in her voice. Clear diction and a calm, unhurried pace, perfect for reading trivia questions aloud to a room. For mischievous moments she can turn playfully sly, but she is never scary. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

## Preview text (the same for every variant, so they are easy to compare)

```
Welcome to Victoria's Merry Quizmas! Tonight, our teams compete for glory... and for prizes. Pick a question from the board, and you have thirty seconds to answer. But watch out for the naughty list... and for Krampus. Here is your question: Under which plant are people traditionally supposed to kiss at Christmas? The answer is... mistletoe! Ho ho ho, well done!
```

## What to listen for

- Every word is clear on laptop speakers and in a room with chatter: questions matter more than mood.
- Warm and friendly, not sleepy: the game lasts an hour.
- "Krampus" sounds playfully sly, not like a horror film.
- No echo, music or noise in the background.
- The accent is light: coworkers should not have to strain to understand it.

If a variant is close but not quite right, change one thing in the description (for example "slightly faster pace", "less husky", "more smile in the voice") and generate again.
