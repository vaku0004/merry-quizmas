# Narrator voice for Big Brain Battle: ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts

Where: ElevenLabs → Voices → Create a voice → Voice Design. Paste a voice description, paste the preview text, generate, listen to all variants, save the best one, and send the Voice ID. The game calls the narrator "Quizmaster Quinn" (changeable in `games/trivia/theme.py`); a good name in ElevenLabs is the same.

## Voice description

### A. TV quiz host (main choice, female)

```
A smart, charming woman in her forties with a clear, warm alto voice and a neutral American accent. She sounds like the host of a popular evening quiz show on TV: quick-witted, encouraging and a little playful, with a smile you can hear. Crisp diction and a confident, easy-to-follow pace for a room full of coworkers. She can build suspense before a reveal and sound genuinely delighted by a right answer. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

### B. TV quiz host (male)

```
A smart, charming man in his forties with a clear, warm baritone and a neutral American accent. He sounds like the host of a popular evening quiz show on TV: quick-witted, encouraging and a little playful, with a smile you can hear. Crisp diction and a confident, easy-to-follow pace for a room full of coworkers. He can build suspense before a reveal and sound genuinely delighted by a right answer. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

## Preview text

```
Welcome to Victoria's Big Brain Battle! Tonight, our teams put their brains to the test, for glory and for prizes. But watch out for the wild card... and for the mystery box! Here is your question: How many hearts does an octopus have? The answer is... three! Brilliant, Team Brainiacs!
```

## What to listen for

- Every word is clear on laptop speakers in a room with chatter.
- Smart and friendly, not pompous.
- Suspense before the answer sounds fun, not dramatic.
- No echo, music or background noise.
