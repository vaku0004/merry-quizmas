# Narrator voice for Sweetheart Showdown: ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts

Where: ElevenLabs → Voices → Create a voice → Voice Design. Paste a voice description, paste the preview text, generate, listen to all variants, save the best one, and send the Voice ID.

The other games have male narrators (a spooky one and a jolly grandfather), so a warm female host gives this game its own character. Option B is a male alternative.

## Voice description

### A. Charming radio host (main choice)

```
A warm, charming woman in her thirties with a smooth, velvety mezzo-soprano voice and a light, neutral American accent. She sounds like a beloved late-night radio host on Valentine's Day: playful, witty and kind, with a smile you can hear and a soft laugh. Clear diction and a relaxed, unhurried pace, easy to follow for a room full of coworkers. She can tease a little and build suspense before a big reveal, but she is never flirty or suggestive, always friendly and classy. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

### B. Classic crooner host (male)

```
A smooth, charming man in his forties with a warm, rich baritone and a light, neutral American accent. He sounds like the host of a classic 1950s TV game show on Valentine's Day: suave, playful and good-humored, with a twinkle in his voice. Clear diction and a relaxed pace, easy to follow for a room full of coworkers. He can build suspense before a reveal and tease a little, but he is never flirty or suggestive, always friendly and classy. Studio-quality recording, close microphone, no background noise.
```

## Preview text

```
Welcome to Victoria's Sweetheart Showdown! Tonight, our teams compete for glory... and for chocolate. Pick a question from the board, and you have thirty seconds to answer. But watch out for Cupid's arrow... and for the box of chocolates. Here is your question: Which winged little figure shoots arrows that make people fall in love? The answer is... Cupid! Well done, Team Lovebirds!
```

## What to listen for

- Every word is clear on laptop speakers in a room with chatter.
- Warm and playful, never flirty: it is an office game.
- Suspense before the answer sounds fun, not dramatic.
- No echo, music or background noise.
